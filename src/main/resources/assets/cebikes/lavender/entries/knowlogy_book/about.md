The {#FF8F40}**Competitive Handbook**{} is your main interface into the competitive system of CE. Allowing you to view a multitude of things pertaining to your competitive status. 

;;;;;

{#FF8F40}**Obtainment - Overview**{}

Obtaining the competitive handbook is easy; you get one when you {#FF8F40}first join the game{}, so you should already have one. If you lose your handbook, or would rather go without one, there are {#FF8F40}two{} other ways you can still access it. Continue to the next page to learn more.

;;;;;

{#FF8F40}**Obtainment - Crafting**{}

<recipe;cobblemonevolved:competitivehandbook>

- You can craft the {#FF8F40}competitive handbook{} with the recipe shown above. If you'd rather go without the item, you can also use {#FF8F40}`/competitivehandbook`{} to bring up the same screen.

;;;;;

{#FF8F40}**GUI - Nameplate**{}

While on the GUI screen, you will be shown all things related to your competitive career.

![Nameplate GUI](cobblemonevolved:textures/knowlegy/knowlegy_nameplate.png)

In the top right corner of the screen you will find your {#FF8F40}nameplate{}, showing your {#FF8F40}tier{}, {#FF8F40}rank{}, and your {#FF8F40}username{}.

;;;;;

{#FF8F40}**GUI - Friendly Battles**{}

![Friendly battles ON](cobblemonevolved:textures/knowlegy/knowlegy_friendlybattleson.png)
![Friendly battles OFF](cobblemonevolved:textures/knowlegy/knowlegy_friendlybattlesoff.png)

On the opposite side of the screen you will find the {#FF8F40}friendly battle buttons{}. You can toggle them by pressing in the buttons underneath to make them {#40FF4D}on{} or {#FF4242}off{}. You will know what the status is based on the colour of the text above the buttons showing the respective colour.
- Note: You {#FF4242}cannot{} battle people with conflicting friendly battle settings. Having friendly battles {#40FF4D}on{} means your rank will not be affected by battles. Having them {#FF4242}off{} means your rank will be affected.

;;;;;

{#FF8F40}**GUI - Ranked Stats**{}

![Current ranked stats](cobblemonevolved:textures/knowlegy/knowlegy_currentstats.png)
![All-time ranked stats](cobblemonevolved:textures/knowlegy/knowlegy_allstats.png)

Underneath the friendly battle buttons you will find your {#FF8F40}ranked stats{}. This shows you all your competitive info for either your {#FF8F40}current season{} stats, or your {#FF8F40}all-time{} stats.
